# Ember Quarry 5:00–6:30: what kills the weak pilot (2026-10-23)

**Short version.** Lateness kills; inaction does not. A pilot that reacts 250 ms late but takes every chance loses 7 of
180 runs and takes 301 damage per run, the same as the 250 ms / every-2nd-chance arm in `weak-pilot.md` (5 of 180,
302). A pilot that reacts instantly but takes only every second chance loses none and takes 126 (default 110; the
difference, +16 ± 15, barely clears zero). In the Quarry window the late pilot loses 84–92 hp per run against 27 for the
default pilot. That damage is two things in roughly equal parts: **wisp lances** (about 40 hp per run, 2.3 hits instead
of 1.0) and **melee contact from brutes and Thornbacks** (about 39–46 hp, 3 hits instead of 0.5). Thornback charges,
ember vents, wisp contact and the boss add almost nothing. The Wild Surge matters: in the Quarry the late pilot loses
1.0 hp per second while the surge is on against 0.38 before it. Wisp *count* alone does not explain it, though: on
average only 1.7 wisps are alive during the surge.

**First hypothesis, checked.** "Wild Surge ramp plus wisp count" is half right. The surge half of the Quarry is where the
damage rate jumps (2.6× for the late pilot, 1.5× for the default pilot), and the lance is the biggest single source.
But melee contact grows just as much. The surge also speeds up waves (`waveInterval` × 0.78), so more brutes and
Thornbacks reach a pilot whose dodging is late. Note the confound: the surge always covers the back 45% of the Quarry, so "surge on"
is also "later in the stage", when lance damage and cooldown have ramped too (`wispLanceDamage`, `wispLanceCooldown`).

**Proposed knob (not applied).** The wisp lance telegraph, `WISP_LANCE_WINDUP` in `src/game/wispLance.ts` (0.95 s
today). It is the one hazard built around reaction time: a 250 ms delay uses up a quarter of the warning. Raising it to
about 1.2 s gives the late pilot back roughly what it loses, leaves melee alone, and should matter less to the default pilot,
which takes about one lance hit in the window (not measured; the gate below checks it). Keep the aim lead at its current value when changing it (`wispLanceTarget` leads by
`speed × WISP_LANCE_WINDUP × 0.68`, capped at 165 px), or a longer telegraph also aims further ahead. If the phone
session shows melee contact rather than lances is what hurts, the second candidate is the surge wave-pace multiplier
(0.78 in `waveInterval`). Direction waits for Daniel's phone run.

**Regression gate for that change.** (1) On this slice, the `off-weakR250N2` arm keeps at least one win in every hero ×
Quarry-route group (today 28–30 of 30 in each) and loses no more than today's 5 of 180. (2) The default pilot (`off`)
still wins 180 of 180 with lowest HP no lower than today's 72.7%. A tuning change cannot leave default runs byte-identical,
because the game itself changes; byte-identity is the gate for this PR's tooling (below), not for the tuning.

**What was run.** The same 180-run slice as `weak-pilot.json` (30 Waylight seeds w1–w75 × 3 heroes × 2 Quarry routes,
Moonfen fixed to Moonflame, no weapon commands), four arms with `--trace damage`: `off` (default), `off-weakR250N2`,
`off-weakR250N1` (`--skill 250`, reaction only) and `off-weakR0N2` (`--skill 0:2`, action rate only). The trace is
read-only: the 360 `off` and `off-weakR250N2` runs match `weak-pilot.json` on every recorded field, and the per-hit trace adds up to
`damageTaken` in all 720 runs (0 hp unattributed).

**Identical-run guard.** With this branch, re-running the 9-seed resonance split (5 arms, 270 runs, no trace)
reproduced `resonance-split-9seeds.json` exactly on every field except wall-clock time and finish timestamp. The only
game-code change is a source label on each `GroveScene.takeDamage` call, which the game ignores.

Bot evidence on desktop Chrome, muted, scene-only fixed 60 Hz steps. Not human or Android evidence.
